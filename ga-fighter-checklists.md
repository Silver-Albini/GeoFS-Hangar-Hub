### **General Aviation & Fighter Jet Checklists**

Quick-action procedures for light single-engine aircraft (**e.g. Cessna 172**) and supersonic fighter jets (**e.g. F-16 Fighting Falcon**).

---

### **Part 1: General Aviation**

#### **1. Preflight & Taxi**
* **Parking Brake:** Verify **ENGAGED** (`Spacebar`).
* **Flaps:** Set to **UP / 0°** (`[`).
* **Taxi:** Release brakes (`Spacebar`), apply **20–25% throttle**, and keep taxi speed under **15 knots**.

#### **2. Takeoff**
* **Flaps:** **0°** for normal runways (or **10° / 1 notch** for short runways).
* **Throttle:** Advance smoothly to **100%** (`9`).
* **Rotate ($V_R$):** At **55–60 knots**, gently pull back (`Down Arrow`) to pitch up to **7°–10°**.
* **Climb:** Maintain **70–75 knots** for best rate of climb ($V_Y$).

#### **3. Cruise**
* **Altitude:** Level off between **3,000 and 8,500 ft**.
* **Throttle:** Set to **70–75%** to cruise at **105–115 knots**.
* **Trim:** Adjust pitch trim until the aircraft holds altitude hands-free.

#### **4. Approach & Landing**
* **Downwind / Base Leg:** Reduce throttle to **40%**, slow below **85 knots**, and deploy **10°–20° flaps** (`]`).
* **Final Approach:** Deploy full flaps (**30°**) and maintain **60–65 knots**.
* **Touchdown:** Reduce throttle to **IDLE (`0`)** just above the runway, flare gently, and apply brakes (`Spacebar`) after touchdown.

---

### **Part 2: Fighter Jet**

#### **1. Preflight & Line-Up**
* **Parking Brake:** Verify **ENGAGED** (`Spacebar`).
* **Airbrakes / Speedbrakes:** Verify **CLOSED** (`B`).
* **Controls Check:** Briefly test pitch, roll, and rudder deflection before taxiing onto the runway.

#### **2. Afterburner Takeoff**
* **Brakes:** Hold (`Spacebar`) while spooling engine to **80%**, then release brakes.
* **Throttle:** Push to **100% Full Afterburner** (`9`).
* **Rotate ($V_R$):** At **150–165 knots**, pull back smoothly to **10°–12°** pitch (avoid over-rotating to prevent a tailstrike).
* **Gear Up:** Retract landing gear (`G`) immediately once airborne and stay below **300 knots** until gear doors are closed.

#### **3. High-Speed Cruise & Maneuvering**
* **Subsonic Cruise:** Set throttle to **75–85%** (military power without afterburner) to cruise at **450–550 knots** and conserve virtual fuel/stability.
* **Supersonic Dash:** Engage **100% throttle** above **25,000 ft** to exceed **Mach 1.2+**.
* **High-G Turns:** Keep speed above **350 knots** during tight turns to maintain energy and avoid stalling.

#### **4. Approach & Landing**
* **Initial Descent:** Reduce throttle to **30–40%** and deploy airbrakes (`B`) to bleed speed below **250 knots**.
* **Gear Down:** Below **250 knots**, lower landing gear (`G`) and retract airbrakes (`B`) if speed drops too fast.
* **Final Approach:** Maintain a **11°–13° Angle of Attack (AoA)** at **145–155 knots**, controlling your descent rate primarily with the throttle.
* **Touchdown:** Touch down smoothly on the main gear, gently lower the nosewheel, deploy airbrakes (`B`), and apply wheel brakes (`Spacebar`) below **100 knots**.
