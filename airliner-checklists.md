### **Commercial Airliner Checklists (e.g. Boeing 737 & Airbus A320)**

Use this step-by-step checklist for realistic commercial flights in GeoFS, from parking gate setup to final shutdown.

---

### **1. Preflight & Startup (At the Gate)**

* **Aircraft & Livery:** Select your airliner (e.g., Boeing 737-800 or Airbus A320) and load your desired airline livery.
* **Parking Brake:** Verify **ENGAGED** (`Spacebar`).
* **Flaps:** Set to **UP / 0°** (`[` until retracted).
* **Autopilot:** Set initial cruise altitude (e.g., `32000` ft) and runway heading on the autopilot panel (keep **AP** switched **OFF** until after takeoff).
* **Camera View:** Switch to Cockpit View (`C`) or Follow View (`V`) to check surrounding traffic.

---

### **2. Taxi to Runway**

* **Parking Brake:** **RELEASE** (`Spacebar`).
* **Throttle:** Increase smoothly to **20–25%** (`Page Up` / `+`).
* **Taxi Speed:** Keep speed below **20 knots** on straight taxiways and **10 knots** during sharp turns.
* **Steering:** Use rudder/nosewheel steering (`A` / `D` or mouse) to stay centered on the yellow line.
* **Hold Short:** Stop before the white hold-short line at the runway threshold and check for landing traffic.

---

### **3. Before Takeoff & Line-Up**

* **Flaps:** Set to **Takeoff position (1–2 notches / ~5°–15°)** (`]`).
* **Spoilers / Airbrakes:** Verify **DISARMED / RETRACTED** (`B`).
* **Trim:** Set elevator trim to neutral (`0`).
* **Line-Up:** Taxi onto the runway, align with the centerline, and hold brakes briefly.

---

### **4. Takeoff & Initial Climb**

* **Brakes:** **RELEASE** (`Spacebar`).
* **Throttle:** Advance smoothly to **100% TO/GA** (`9` or `Page Up`).
* **Rotate ($V_R$):** At **140–150 knots**, gently pull back on the stick (`Down Arrow`) to pitch up to **10°–15°**.
* **Positive Rate:** Once climbing steadily, retract landing gear (`G`).
* **Flaps Up:** Above **180–200 knots** and **1,000 ft AGL**, retract flaps (`[`).
* **Autopilot:** Engage **AP** (`A` on panel), **ALT**, and **HDG / NAV** once stabilized.

---

### **5. Cruise**

* **Altitude:** Level off at cruise altitude (typically **30,000–38,000 ft**).
* **Speed:** Maintain **280–310 knots IAS** (around Mach 0.78–0.80).
* **Fuel & Navigation:** Monitor flight path on the NAV map and prepare your Top of Descent (TOD) roughly **90–100 NM** before the destination airport.

---

### **6. Descent & Approach**

* **Descent:** Reduce throttle to **40–50%** and set Autopilot Vertical Speed (`V/S`) to **-1,500 to -2,000 ft/min**.
* **Below 10,000 ft:** Slow down to **250 knots** or less.
* **Initial Approach (~15 NM out):** Reduce speed to **180–200 knots** and deploy first notch of flaps (`]`).
* **Final Approach (~8 NM out):**
  * Align with the runway localizer (or engage **ILS**).
  * Lower landing gear (`G`).
  * Deploy full landing flaps (`]`).
  * Stabilize approach speed at **135–145 knots**.

---

### **7. Landing & Shutdown**

* **Disengage Autopilot:** Turn off **AP** at **500–1,000 ft AGL** for manual flare.
* **Flare:** At **30–50 ft** above the runway, reduce throttle to **IDLE (`0`)** and gently raise the nose by **3°–5°**.
* **Touchdown:** Once main wheels touch down, deploy spoilers (`B`) and engage reverse thrust (`-` / `Page Down`).
* **Braking:** Apply wheel brakes (`Spacebar`) below **80 knots** and vacate the runway onto a taxiway.
* **Shutdown:** Taxi to the gate, engage parking brake (`Spacebar`), retract flaps (`[`), and set throttle to **0%**.
