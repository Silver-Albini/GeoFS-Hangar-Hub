### **Complete GeoFS Fleet & Liveries Showcase**

A comprehensive guide to custom liveries, paint schemes, and recommended setups for all **33 official GeoFS aircraft & vehicles** using the **LiverySelector** add-on.

---

### **How to Load Custom Liveries**

1. Install **Tampermonkey** in your browser and enable the **GeoFS LiverySelector** script (available in my [GeoFS Best Add-ons](https://github.com/Silver-Albini/GeoFS-Best-Addons) repository).
2. Select any of the 33 aircraft or vehicles below inside **GeoFS**.
3. Click the **Livery** icon on the left sidebar menu and choose from the community presets or paste a custom texture URL.
