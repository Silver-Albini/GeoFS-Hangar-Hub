### **Community-Contributed Aircraft & Liveries Guide**

A complete catalog of **71 community-built aircraft and specialty vehicles** available in **GeoFS** (via the *Community Contributed* menu), including top livery recommendations and flight highlights.

---

### **How to Access Community Aircraft**

1. Open **GeoFS** and click **Aircraft** in the bottom menu bar.
2. Scroll down to the **Community Contributed** section to select any of the aircraft listed below.
3. Use the **LiverySelector** add-on (available in my [GeoFS Best Add-ons](https://github.com/Silver-Albini/GeoFS-Best-Addons) repository) to swap between custom airline and military paint schemes.
